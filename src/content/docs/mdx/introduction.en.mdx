Kense is not another chat window. It places agents inside a working system that can execute, recover, reuse, and be constrained by the organization. The product has two layers — Kense Work and Kense OS — that share one intent and permission contract.

<h2 id="two-layers">Two layers, not two silos</h2>

- Kense Work: a local-first workspace. Models, files, and tools compose on your machine; sensitive context can stay on-device.
- Kense OS: the control plane and durable runtime. It orchestrates intent, stores memory, publishes policy, and resumes after interruption.

<Callout>
Finish one real task first, then decide what belongs in OS. Do not invert the order: cloud first, use case later.
</Callout>

<h2 id="for-whom">Who this is for</h2>

If an agent must touch real files, repositories, or internal systems, a one-shot conversation is not enough. Kense is for teams that need boundaries, audit, and recoverable execution: platform engineering, knowledge work, delivery, and regulated environments.
