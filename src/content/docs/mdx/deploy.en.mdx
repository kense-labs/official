Where it runs is a choice. Working semantics should not fork. Deployment is a risk and operations question, not a second agent protocol.

<h2 id="modes">Three landings</h2>

- Managed OS: Kense hosts the control plane for a fast start.
- BYOC: the control plane runs in your cloud account and VPC.
- Self-hosted: you own deploy, security, and upgrades.

<h2 id="plans">How the subscription unfolds</h2>

1. Free: one Work seat and a first local session.
2. Builder: full Work plus metered OS.
3. Team: sharing, policy publish, and flow automation.
4. Enterprise: dedicated or self-hosted, SSO / SCIM, custom SLA.

Work and OS are not sold separately. Read-only collaborators use included seats. Paid seats are people who use Work or manage OS flows and policy.
